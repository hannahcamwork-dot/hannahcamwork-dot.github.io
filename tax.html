How a T1 file moves

  1. 01

    Client documents

    Slips, receipts and last year’s Notice of Assessment come in and get sorted before anything is entered.

  2. 02

    Review

    Check for anything missing, any eligibility questions, and any compliance risks.

  3. 03

    Validation

    Match slips to the client’s information. Check RRSP receipts, medical expenses and other claims against their support.

  4. 04

    T1 preparation

    Prepare the return in ProFile or UFile, with the deductions, credits and benefits the client is eligible for.

  5. 05

    Cross-check

    Compare figures back to the source documents. Trace anything that doesn’t add up, and prepare support for senior review.

  6. 06

    Filing†

    Finalize the return for filing with the CRA and walk the client through the result.

†After assessment. When the Notice of Assessment comes back, compare it with the filed return. If anything differs, trace it to the source documents and update the file.

A simplified view, based on my work with the CRA volunteer program and at AccTax Canada. In practice the steps overlap.
