About

About me

I’m working toward a Bachelor of Commerce (Honours) in Accounting, with a minor in Finance, at Ontario Tech University. Outside class, I’ve prepared tax returns as a CRA volunteer, done bookkeeping on a co-op term, and handled the finances for a student organization.

01

A little more context

I’ve worked alongside school since 2020: at various bubble tea stores, as a volunteer with the CRA’s CVITP, and on a co-op term at an accounting firm. A lot of what I never learn in class, I’ve already run into at work.

Ideas like completeness, occurrence and reliable evidence made sense to me first as everyday questions. Is every slip here? Does this receipt actually support the claim? Why doesn’t the assessment match what was filed? Bookkeeping at AccTax added a few more: why doesn’t this reconciliation close, and which transaction is causing it?

What I like is that the work has to hold up for someone else. A return or a reconciliation isn’t really finished until another person can pick it up, and trust it.

02

Where I’m headed

I’m aiming for public accounting, starting in tax. It started with volunteering: I joined the CRA’s Community Volunteer Tax Program in 2023, then spent my 2025 co-op term doing tax at AccTax Canada. By the time I took my first taxation course, a lot of it already felt familiar.

I like that tax is specific. There’s a rule, there’s a document, and they either line up or they don’t. It also matters to people directly: a missing slip or an unclaimed credit changes someone’s refund.

Later on I’d like experience in audit, financial reporting and advisory work too, and I plan to pursue the CPA designation after I graduate.
