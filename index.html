01Approach

Four habits I rely on, drawn from tax and bookkeeping work.

How I approach the work

  1. 01

    Document first

    A figure is only as reliable as the slip, receipt or statement behind it. I gather and organize the source documents before anything is entered, so the work starts from evidence rather than assumption.

  2. 02

    Trace the difference

    When a return doesn’t agree with the Notice of Assessment, or the books don’t agree with the bank, the difference is telling you something. I follow it back to the source instead of adjusting the number until it fits.

  3. 03

    Communicate clearly

    Most clients don’t need the section of the Income Tax Act. They need to know why a credit applies to them, what is still missing, and what happens next. Technical work should still make sense to the person receiving it.

  4. 04

    Check the details

    Accuracy isn’t a step saved for the end. Matching slips as they’re entered and reconciling on a regular schedule is what keeps a small error small.
