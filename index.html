01

Why tax?

It started with volunteering. I joined the CRA’s Community Volunteer Income Tax Program in 2023, stayed for three filing seasons, and then spent my co-op term doing tax at AccTax Canada. When I took my first taxation course, a lot of it already felt familiar.

What I like about tax is that it’s specific. There’s a rule, there’s a document, and they either line up or they don’t. When they don’t, I want to know why.

It also matters to people in a very direct way. A missing slip or an unclaimed credit changes someone’s refund, and sometimes what they decide to do next year. I want to do work people can rely on, and tax is where I’ve seen that most clearly.

— Hannah

02

Habits I picked up from tax and bookkeeping work.

How I approach the work

  1. 01

    Document first

    A number is only as good as the slip or receipt behind it, so I get the documents in order before I enter anything.

  2. 02

    Trace the difference

    If a return doesn’t match the Notice of Assessment, or the books don’t match the bank, something is off. I’d rather find out what than adjust the number until it fits.

  3. 03

    Communicate clearly

    Clients don’t need me to quote the Income Tax Act. They want to know why they qualify for a credit, what’s still missing, and what happens next.

  4. 04

    Check the details

    I check as I go, matching slips while I enter them and reconciling regularly, instead of saving it all for the end. Small errors are easier to fix while they’re small.
